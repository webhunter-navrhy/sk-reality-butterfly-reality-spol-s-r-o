Beautiful Spacious 4-Bedroom Duplex with Two Terraces, Fireplace & Garage – Quiet Green Area Slanec, Krasňany

Butterfly-reality is proud to offer exclusively on behalf of the owner this beautiful and spacious 4-bedroom duplex apartment with two terraces, a fireplace, and a garage parking space, located in the quiet and green Slanec area of Krasňany, Bratislava.

APARTMENT

Floor area: 145 m²

Terraces 2×

Floor: 4th of 4 (top floor)

Layout:

4th floor: entrance hall, spacious living room with fireplace and access to the terrace, kitchen with dining area, bedroom with en-suite bathroom, separate toilet 3rd floor: two separate bedrooms with access to a balcony, large bathroom, storage spaces

Orientation: dual aspect – bright and airy apartment

Parking: garage parking space in the basement + storage room

RESIDENTIAL BUILDING

Final approval (built): 2008

Construction: brick + reinforced concrete

Elevator

Quiet, well-maintained building in a small residential community

LOCATION – Slanec, Krasňany

The apartment building is situated on the edge of the vineyards, in an exceptionally quiet and safe part of Krasňany.

The location offers beautiful views of the Small Carpathians, plenty of greenery, and direct access to nature – an ideal environment for families, runners, and cyclists.

Within walking distance, you’ll find Horáreň Krasňany, featuring a children’s playground, BBQ and relaxation area, as well as asphalt cycling paths and forest trails in the nearby Carpathian woods.
